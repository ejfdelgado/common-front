6. Camera and Body Detection Using MediaPipe

The camera is used as input for the MediaPipe body detection model in order to identify body reference points (landmarks) and estimate the poses or movements needed to interact with the game.

According to the intended operation of this beta version, each frame is processed to perform detection and then discarded; camera frames are not stored as part of this feature. The only personal data the Platform stores persistently is the user’s email address, in order to identify who created each game and to assign or verify special editing permissions over certain parts of the game.

Detection is experimental and may produce incorrect, incomplete, or inconsistent results due to lighting, framing, clothing, body position, the camera, the browser, or limitations of the model. The browser may request permission to access the camera; if the user denies it, certain features may not be available.
