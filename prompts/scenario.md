Create a **stylized low-poly cartoon 3D environment asset** for a colorful, age-neutral, browser-based interactive world.

**ART DIRECTION**
- Combine clean low-poly geometry with soft, expressive cartoon shapes.
- Use simplified, recognizable silhouettes with playful but sophisticated proportions.
- Avoid photorealism, excessive detail, childish exaggeration, and realistic surface imperfections.
- Maintain a cohesive, welcoming visual identity suitable for educational adventures, wellness experiences, and entertainment.

**GEOMETRY AND SHAPES**
- Construct objects from simple geometric primitives: beveled cubes, low-resolution spheres, cylinders, cones, and faceted organic shapes.
- Prefer broad, clearly defined forms over intricate geometry.
- Use moderate polygon faceting, softened silhouettes, and intentionally simplified topology.
- Keep small details integrated into larger geometric shapes.
- Use solid volumes rather than complex layered surfaces.
- Avoid thin, fragile, or excessively intricate elements.
- Ensure the silhouette remains recognizable from a distance.

**COLOR PALETTE**
- Use harmonious, moderately saturated colors with a warm, optimistic atmosphere.
- Prefer soft pastel-influenced colors combined with selected vibrant accents.
- Limit each object to approximately 3–5 coordinated colors, excluding subtle shading variations.
- Use color blocks to define major shapes instead of complex textures.
- Suggested environmental palette:
  - Forest green: #6FAE65
  - Light green: #A5C96A
  - Sky blue: #70B8ED
  - Turquoise: #54C5C1
  - Warm sand: #E9C58D
  - Stone lavender-gray: #9C94B6
  - Warm wood brown: #A97550
  - Soft coral: #E78D91
- Maintain consistent color relationships across all assets while allowing biome-specific variations.

**MATERIALS AND SURFACES**
- Use predominantly matte, nonmetallic materials.
- Prefer solid colors or subtle gradients over photographic textures.
- Avoid realistic wood grain, bark, detailed leaf veins, dirt, scratches, and procedural noise.
- Use minimal specular highlights and avoid reflective or transparent materials unless functionally necessary.
- Keep materials simple enough for efficient real-time WebGL rendering.

**PROPORTIONS AND SCALE**
- Use a consistent world scale where 1 unit equals 1 meter.
- Preserve recognizable real-world proportions, allowing controlled cartoon exaggeration.
- Favor broad, rounded, or faceted masses over narrow, highly detailed shapes.
- Exaggerate important identifying features moderately, approximately 10–25%, when it improves readability.
- Maintain consistent proportions between assets of the same category.
- Define each asset's dimensions explicitly in meters.

**LIGHTING AND PRESENTATION**
- Use soft, bright, neutral daylight for asset previews.
- Show simple geometric shading that emphasizes volume and polygon facets.
- Avoid dramatic cinematic lighting, heavy ambient occlusion, bloom, and complex shadows.
- Keep lighting consistent between assets so colors remain comparable.

**PERFORMANCE CONSTRAINTS**
- Design for lightweight browser-based 3D rendering.
- Minimize polygon count, draw calls, materials, and texture dependencies.
- Favor reusable and instanced geometry.
- Avoid volumetric clouds, particle-heavy effects, complex foliage, and expensive shaders.
- Preserve visual appeal through silhouettes, color composition, and proportions rather than geometric detail.

**ASSET PRESENTATION**
- Generate a single isolated 3D object.
- Show the entire object in a three-quarter orthographic view.
- Use a neutral background with even lighting.
- Do not include characters, text, logos, interface elements, or unrelated decorative objects.
- Keep the object visually consistent with other assets created using this style guide.
